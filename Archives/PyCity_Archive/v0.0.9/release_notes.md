## v0.0.9

**New:**
- Farms, factories, and houses are now split across two resource chains — Grain/Bread and Timber/Furniture — shown as a small colored dot on each building. Trucks only haul goods within the same chain, so a healthy city needs a mix of both, not just "more farms."
- Zoom in and out with the mouse wheel (or +/-), centered on wherever you're looking.

**Changed:**
- Rebalanced house tax, crime growth, and theft frequency — an uncovered house was previously able to drain money faster than any single house could ever tax back.
- Road congestion clears a bit faster once trucks move on.

**Save compatibility:**
This update changes the save format again (buildings now track which resource chain they're on). Old saves won't load directly — convert yours first: [Update Your Saves](https://pycity.pages.dev/save_convert)
